### annual-spend-trend

> How has total annual spending changed from 2008 to 2026?

```sql
SELECT fiscal_year, ROUND(total_spend, 2) AS total_spend, transaction_count
FROM summary_annual_spend
WHERE fiscal_year BETWEEN 2008 AND 2026
ORDER BY fiscal_year ASC
```

Total annual spending rose from $244.8M in FY2008 to $600.2M in FY2026, about 2.5 times the starting level.

The path was uneven:
- 2008 to 2015: spending stayed flat to lower, peaking at $278.9M in FY2009 and falling to a low of $210.3M in FY2013.
- 2016 to 2020: spending recovered to between $233.0M and $273.1M, with $273.1M in FY2019.
- 2021 to 2022: spending jumped to $374.6M and then $411.6M.
- 2023: spending dropped sharply to $277.7M.
- 2024 to 2026: spending reached $541.5M in FY2024 and $643.7M in FY2025 (the peak), then eased to $600.2M in FY2026.

The most unusual movement is the FY2023 dip followed by the FY2024 surge, which nearly doubled spending in one year ($277.7M to $541.5M). Transaction counts rose far less than dollars: FY2026 has 164,760 transactions against 129,647 in FY2008, a rise of about 27%, so the average payment is much larger now.
